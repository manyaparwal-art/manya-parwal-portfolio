Why Mamarise exists

Women don't live one life at a time.

Parenting over here. Career somewhere else. Wellness in another tab. Community, if she can find one.

A woman can be a mother, a professional, a partner, a caregiver, and still an individual with her own goals, questions and need for support. But most digital experiences treat every part of that life as a completely separate problem.

mother professional individual still becoming
The thought behind it

One woman.
Many roles.

I kept coming back to one simple thought. Why should every part of her life live in a different place? Mamarise became my answer to that, one space where she can find support for what matters to her right now.

What if all these parts could belong in one place?

One idea.
Two different experiences.

I didn't want the website and app to simply repeat each other. They had different jobs to do, so I designed them around different moments in the user's journey.

Giving Mamarise a face

I wanted the logo to feel like a small moment of being held.

When I started working on the identity, I knew I didn't want something that felt too clinical or like another generic wellness brand.

Mamarise is about women, motherhood, growth and having support around you, so the logo had to carry some of that feeling too.

The mother and child became the heart of the mark. The circle around them brings in protection, while the leaves represent growth and new beginnings. Nothing too complicated. Just the things Mamarise is really built around.

Love & Togetherness Growth & Wellbeing Care & Nurture Protection & Support
Building the visual language

Making it feel like Mamarise.

Once the logo started feeling right, everything else had to follow the same feeling. Warm, calm and reassuring, but never too soft or predictable.
