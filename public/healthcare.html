Selected Healthcare Work · 04

Designing healthcare to feel a little less clinical.

During my time at Zarle, I worked across healthcare websites for doctors and medical practices. Different specialities, different people, but the same challenge kept coming back, how do you make important information easier to find and less overwhelming?

Healthcare Web Design UI/UX Client Work
The work

Two live websites. Two very different kinds of trust.

One speaks to women looking for clarity around their health. The other speaks to people dealing with pain, injuries and movement. Designing both made me think more carefully about what people actually need from a healthcare website before they decide to take the next step.

Looking back

What healthcare changed in the way I design.

Working on healthcare made me pay more attention to the quiet decisions. The things that do not necessarily look impressive in a screenshot, but make a real difference when someone is trying to understand what to do next.

01

Clarity comes first

A clever interaction means very little if someone cannot quickly understand what a doctor treats or how to book an appointment.

02

Trust lives in small details

Typography, spacing, hierarchy and tone quietly decide whether a website feels reassuring or confusing.

03

Different doctors need different personalities

Healthcare does not have to look the same everywhere. A women's health website and an orthopaedic website can feel completely different while still being clear and trustworthy.

04

The next step should never feel hidden

Whether it is booking an appointment, understanding a treatment or finding the clinic, people should not have to search for what to do next.

A final thought

Healthcare is one of those spaces where design can quietly make someone feel a little less lost.
